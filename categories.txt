# Email Classification Categories
# Format: CategoryName: Description
#
# Descriptions define the ESSENCE of each category — what makes it uniquely
# itself. The LLM should classify based on the primary nature of the email,
# not by matching sender names or checking exclusion lists.

Personal: A real person writing to you personally. The sender is an individual
using their own email address and the message is direct human correspondence.
Highest priority — takes precedence over all other categories.

ECCG: Work on the Electric City Community Grocery cooperative development project.
The email is about planning, financing, or coordinating the co-op startup, not
general food or community news.

Community: Announcements and updates from a local community organization or
association you belong to. The sender is an organization (not an individual),
and the message is about shared community business or events.

Wellness: Communications from a yoga studio, fitness center, or personal wellness
service provider about classes, memberships, or appointments. The service is
physical wellness, not medical care.

Healthcare: Communications from the formal medical system — doctors, hospitals,
pharmacies, or health insurers — about your medical care, test results, or
health coverage.

Financial: From a financial institution about your money. The sender's primary
business is managing, advising on, or reporting financial assets — banks,
brokerages, tax preparers, investment advisors.

AccountSecurity: A security action is required or has occurred. Two-factor codes,
login alerts, password resets, and suspicious activity warnings. The email exists
because account access is at stake.

OnlineServices: An online platform notifying you about your account — status
changes, billing cycles, policy updates, or service maintenance. The sender is
a digital service provider managing an ongoing relationship.

TechSupport: An active support ticket you opened is being worked on. The email
is a response to a problem you reported and contains resolution progress.

Receipts: Payment has been received or is due. The email's primary purpose is
confirming a completed financial transaction or presenting an invoice.

Orders: A physical or digital order is moving through fulfillment. Shipping
confirmations, tracking updates, and delivery notifications.

Shopping: A retailer or maker community is showing you products to consider
buying. The email is about product discovery or availability, not a transaction
already underway.

Promotions: A company is offering you an incentive or requesting your feedback.
Sales, discounts, loyalty rewards, review requests, and satisfaction surveys.

Events: A specific gathering is happening at a date and time, and you are being
invited or updated. The email exists because of a scheduled occasion.

Travel: A trip you are taking has a confirmation or update. The email is tied to
a specific journey — flights, accommodation, or ground transportation.

SocialMedia: A social or community platform is notifying you of activity on your
account. The sender is the platform itself, not the person who triggered the event.

Newsletters: You subscribed to receive this content on an ongoing basis. The
email's purpose is to deliver information or perspective, not to sell or transact.

Career: An employment opportunity or career-development interaction. Recruiting
outreach, interview scheduling, application status, and professional opportunity.

LocalPolitics: A political campaign, elected official, or civic advocacy
organization is communicating with you about political or governmental matters.

GitHub: A software development event on a git hosting platform. Repository
activity, pull requests, issues, CI results, or integrated security scanning.

RKRoll: Business correspondence addressed to RKRoll.com as a company. The email
is about the company's standing, registration, or operations as a legal entity.
